Conclusion — Key Takeaways from the Paper

  • Geometric Distribution shows how skill (p) determines how quickly a player wins points.
  • Exponential Distribution shows how fatigue (λ) causes energy to decline over match time.
  • Combined Model proves that winning probability is NOT constant — it decreases with time due to fatigue.
  • Players with low λ (slow fatigue) maintain performance longer and have better late-match winning chances.
  • Endurance can override skill in long matches.
